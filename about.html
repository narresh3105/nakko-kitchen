01 / OUR STORY

A Love for Food, Brought Together.

NAKKO Kitchen & Co. is a fictional modern fusion restaurant concept created for our Basic Web Development group project.

Our concept is inspired by the idea that food is more than something we eat. It brings people together, creates connections and turns ordinary moments into memorable experiences.

We bring together familiar favourites, creative flavours and a welcoming dining atmosphere to create a restaurant identity that celebrates good food and great company.

The name NAKKO represents our group spirit: four names coming together to build one shared idea.
